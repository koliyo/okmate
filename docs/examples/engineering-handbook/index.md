# Engineering handbook

Practice library organized by reader questions. Types follow the job of
the record; folders follow the topic. Dated research stays research.
Product contracts stay in the product archive, not here.

Frozen reader questions (do not silently retarget these):

* How should I steer delegated work? → [Task steering](agentic-development/task-steering.md)
* How should I evaluate agent results? → [Evaluate agent results](agentic-development/evaluate-results.md)
* How do I restore a development environment? → [Restore from a manifest](developer-environments/restore-from-manifest.md)
* Where did these conclusions come from? → [Source register](reference/source-register.md)
* What was observed on this machine? → [Backup snapshot](audits/backup-snapshot.md)
* Where is the owning product contract? → Not this corpus. Open the product
  work archive (`docs/examples/software-archive`) at Architecture →
  System overview. Do not copy those facts into this handbook.
